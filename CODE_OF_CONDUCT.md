# Code of conduct

## In short

Be decent. Assume the other person is acting in good faith. Criticise the code,
never the person writing it.

## Expected

- Give the reason behind a disagreement, not just the verdict.
- Accept that a patch can be refused on scope grounds without that being a
  judgement on your ability.
- Respect a decision once it is made and explained, even if you would have
  chosen otherwise.

## Not accepted

- Insults, personal attacks, or harassment of any kind, public or private.
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwanted attention.
- Publishing someone's private information without their explicit permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions, or deliberate bad faith.

## Scope

This applies to every space the project occupies — issues, pull requests,
commits, discussions — and to anyone representing the project elsewhere.

## Reporting

Report a problem to **cherridsaid@gmail.com**. Reports are handled privately.
The maintainer will not name a reporter without their consent.

Consequences range from a warning to a permanent ban, depending on severity and
on whether the behaviour repeats. A maintainer who breaks these rules is held to
them the same way — the rules are not a privilege of the person who wrote them.
